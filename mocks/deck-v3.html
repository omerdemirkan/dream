Dream · UI direction

Deck v3

Decisions first. Dream opens on whatever needs you — one card at a time, from every project, answerable right there. Tap any phone to use it, or open this page on your phone for the full-screen prototype.

  • Color means “needs you.” Amber appears only on a question that is waiting. Everything else is black, white, and gray.
  • A project has two tabs. Status is the live roadmap. Chat holds only what Dream chose to send, and what you send.
  • The portal is a message, expanded. Questions and access requests live in Chat. Tap one and it grows into the full-screen view; Back lands on that exact message.
