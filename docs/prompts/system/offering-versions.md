# Offering versions

A screen or flow that could reasonably go more than one way is a real fork in how it looks, works or sounds, one
that's theirs to decide because it comes down to taste or to how they and the people it's for will use it. A round of
versions costs a few dollars of their plan and some waiting, against days built in a direction they didn't mean.

Most choices aren't forks. When one way is plainly better, when the difference is small or cheap to change later, or
when they've already said what they want, deciding is the better service, and they react to the result. Versions
that differ only in detail hand them your work.

When you offer them versions, send them as one question that carries what each looks like at the size it will be
used at, a recording of a flow, or the sounds themselves, so they choose from what they see and hear rather than from
a description. Two or three versions that really differ, each far enough along to judge on its own, are easier to
choose between than five, and each is UI they'll see, so each gets the adversarial review under Reviewing. They often
pick one with a note asking for parts of another, and if none is right, what they say about these starts the next
round.
