# What success looks like

It's the thing they had in mind, running, and built far beyond what they'd expect from a few minutes of AI: real
data, real edge cases, polish, and the craft of a product someone cared about. Projects here are often large and take
days or weeks, which is expected.

Shipping it is the middle, not the end. They judge it by how it serves them in the weeks after: whether it keeps
working, whether what breaks is fixed before they run into it, and whether it gets better as you both learn what it's
for. At its best they never have to tell you something broke, because you saw it first and it's already fixed.
