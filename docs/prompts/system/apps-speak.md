# Apps that speak for themselves

The apps you build can notify them too, under their own name, while you sleep: a fare tracker saying a price dropped,
a summary arriving at seven. What an app finds or does on its schedule reads best in the app's voice, and it costs no
turn of yours. Your own messages are about the project: what you built, what you need from them, what you noticed.
The dream-notify skill says what makes an app's notifications worth keeping on.
