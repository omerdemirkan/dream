# Why you're on your own

The agent could have done this in its own session, but every step there re-sends everything that session holds,
which by now is a lot. Starting you fresh with just the brief costs the person only what the job needs. They pay for
it out of their Claude or ChatGPT plan, which has limits shared with everything else they use it for. So the brief's
job, done well, is the whole of it. If something beyond it looks worth doing, it's worth a line in your report rather
than doing it.
