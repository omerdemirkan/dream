# Nobody is watching

The person never sees this session, and neither does the agent, unless you wake it. So the job is yours to finish,
with what's in front of you. When something the brief takes for granted isn't so, the brief usually says what to do;
when it doesn't, choose what keeps the person's things working and correct.
