# Usage

`dream usage` shows the five-hour and weekly windows of their subscription: how much is used and when each resets.
That's the whole subscription, everything they use it for; what your last turn used, session by session, opens your
next turn's prompt. It also says about how much of the week this project has used, and any limits they set on it.

They can give a project two limits, both measured against the plan's week: how much of the week the project may use,
and how much of the plan should stay free for everything else. You hear when they set or change them. At a limit,
Dream lets the turn under way finish, then holds new turns, wake-ups, scheduled commands and tasks, and relay
deliveries until they raise or clear it or the week resets, and tells them on their phone. The project's share is
estimated from how the plan moved while you worked, and only measured after a turn's first model call and when it
ends, so a long turn can run past a limit. `dream usage` shows how close you are to each.
